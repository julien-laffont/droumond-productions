# Prototype: provisional visual direction (#5)

Throwaway prototype for issue #5. It lives only on the branch `prototype/visual-direction` and is never merged into `develop` or `main`. The decisions it shows are recorded in the comment "Decisões antes do protótipo" on issue #5; the real site is built later, in Astro, from those decisions.

## What it shows

The whole home page (first screen, Projetos, Sobre, Equipa, filming strip, Contacto, footer), in the direction Nal chose on 2026-11-16:

- **Variant C** (after Rodeo FX) for the type and the first screen: Archivo in several widths, light canvas with the background video, the logo revealed at the centre. Under the name, as on Rodeo FX, words pass one at a time (Séries, Suspense, Terror, Filmagens) and "Produtora audiovisual" stays.
- **The Mill** for Projects: an asymmetric 2:1 grid with the title under each image. No large statement above it and no brush stroke under section titles. Sobre mirrors it: small text on the left, a wide still on the right.

Variants A (Editorial, after The Mill) and B (List, after Oats Studios) were dropped.

## How to view it

It is published as a claude.ai Artifact (link on issue #5). `index.html` has no doctype on purpose: the Artifact wraps it in its own `<html>`/`<head>`/`<body>`. Opening it straight from disk works, but in quirks mode.

## Files

- `index.html`: the page, with its CSS and JS inline.
- `fonts/`: Archivo, open-license (SIL OFL 1.1, license next to it), latin subset, from Fontsource.
- `assets/logo.png`: the logo symbol, converted from Carlos's JPEG (349 px, black on white) to ink with transparency. A vector file (SVG) from Carlos would be sharper.
- `assets/efeito-placebo.jpg`: the teaser's YouTube thumbnail, letterbox cropped.
- `assets/mini-serie.jpg`: the mini-series poster from Instagram.
- `assets/equipa-1.jpg` to `equipa-5.jpg`, `assets/sobre.jpg`: character photos from the Instagram profile, cropped to leave out the app's icons and the names written on the posters. The team gave consent for the use of their image.
- `assets/bg.mp4`, `assets/bg-poster.jpg`: background video generated by code (brushstrokes on canvas) and its still frame.
