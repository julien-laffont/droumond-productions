Droumond Productions is an audiovisual production company based in Leiria, founded in 2026. We write, direct and produce fiction for the screen and for social media: _Efecto Placebo_, a thriller mini-series, is in production, and _Resident Evil_, on Instagram, tells a horror story in five short episodes.

With the same crew and equipment, we also film for clients: commercials, brand videos and music videos, in the Leiria region.
