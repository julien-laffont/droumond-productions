Leiria wakes up to the news: Carlos Droumond, a Panamanian musician the public adores, has disappeared. His car is found locked in a pine forest near Marinha Grande, with no sign of an accident. Julien Laffont, his best friend, a methodical Frenchman who sees patterns where others see chance, knows more than he lets on. Days earlier, Carlos handed him a notebook holding the so-called Partituras Divinas, music a Coimbra professor kept hidden and that was never meant to be played. Carlos played it. And the professor is dead.

With Inara, Carlos’s blunt, guarded friend who carries an old loss, Julien investigates on his own while the police suspect him. Someone is watching them, and someone is willing to kill for those pages. Between logic and the inexplicable, _Efecto Placebo_ is a suspense series about the power of music, and about what we choose to believe.
