# Droumond Productions website

The public website of Droumond Productions, a Portuguese audiovisual production company. It shows the company's work and brings in client contacts.

## Language

**Project**:
An audiovisual work produced by Droumond Productions, such as the mini-series _Efecto Placebo_, or _Resident Evil_.
_Avoid_: work, portfolio item, production

**Filming service**:
Droumond Productions hired, with its crew and equipment, to film for another person or company.
_Avoid_: recording, shoot, hire, gravação

**Team member**:
One of the company's two co-founders, Carlos Droumond and Julien Laffont. They also act in the Projects, but stay Team members.
_Avoid_: staff, crew, cast

**Cast member**:
A person who plays a Character in a Project and is not a Team member. Together they are the Elenco.
_Avoid_: team member, actor list

**Character**:
A fictional role in a Project, played by a Cast member or a Team member.
_Avoid_: team member, persona
