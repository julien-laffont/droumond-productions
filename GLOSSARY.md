# Droumond Productions website

The public website of Droumond Productions, a Portuguese audiovisual production company. It shows the company's work and brings in client contacts.

## Language

**Project**:
An audiovisual work produced by Droumond Productions, such as the mini-series _Efecto Placebo_ or _Resident Evil_, on Instagram.
_Avoid_: work, portfolio item, production

**Filming service**:
Droumond Productions hired, with its crew and equipment, to film for another person or company.
_Avoid_: recording, shoot, hire, gravação

**Team member**:
A real person who works with Droumond Productions.
_Avoid_: character, cast, staff

**Character**:
A fictional role in a Project, such as the ones on the company's Instagram profile.
_Avoid_: team member, persona
